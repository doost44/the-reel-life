Click the prototype once after it opens, so the browser lets it speak.

Start it on the exhibit computer first

The prototype runs on that computer, with its cameras, so these buttons only work there. In the project folder, run:

.\run.ps1 -OpenScreens

That also opens the exhibit fullscreen on its monitor with sound on, so no click is needed. Use this for the show.

If the exhibit doesn't open, its monitor isn't connected or has changed. Go to Setup, choose the monitor and press Open fullscreen.
