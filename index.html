The Reel Life

A live exhibit about watching reels. Put your phone on the platform and watch with something that has never seen one.

Run it here, in Chrome

Open the prototype
Look Setup

Nothing to install: the eye runs in the browser, with this computer's cameras. Use Chrome or Edge.

The first time, open Setup, press Refresh devices and allow the cameras (and window management, to open screens on other monitors). Then choose the phone camera and the face camera. Keep one page of it open: the first one opened runs the eye.

Click the prototype once after it opens, so it goes fullscreen and the browser lets it speak.

Or with the Python eye

On a computer running the Python eye these open it instead, with the visitor's QR code and footage recording. In the project folder, run:

.\run.ps1 -OpenScreens

(on a Mac, ./run.sh --open-screens). That also opens the exhibit fullscreen on its monitor with sound on, so no click is needed.

Prototype Setup

If the exhibit doesn't open, its monitor isn't connected or has changed. Go to Setup, choose the monitor and press Open fullscreen.
